Prompt exécuté le 14 octobre 2026. Aucun mode CLI/API.

Use case: photorealistic-natural. Asset type: seamless square albedo texture for Mars terrain in a first-person survival game, covering 4 by 4 meters. Primary request: create a production quality flat base-color map of dry dusty Martian regolith, muted ochre tan clay and rusty brown fines, scattered small angular grey-brown basalt fragments 1 to 8 cm, rare flat embedded stones 15 cm, subtle irregular wind-swept fine grit patches. Orthographic straight down, entire frame surface. Diffuse perfectly even lighting, no directional shadows, no ambient occlusion, no specular highlights, no horizon, no perspective. Low contrast natural dusty earthy palette guided by NASA Curiosity natural-color imagery, avoid bright red orange saturation. No objects besides regolith, no tracks, no text, no watermark, no large boulders. Tile seamlessly on both horizontal and vertical edges; no border, uniform density, no identifiable repeated landmark. Output one square 1024x1024 texture, artistic interpretation not scientific data.

Le fichier reçu fait réellement 1254² ; conservé sans modification comme maître.
L'export/raccord et les mipmaps sont préparés par le script, pas par le jeu.
